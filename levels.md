# Pet Match levels

**Pet Match: Zoo Animal Puzzle has 200 levels, and every level is a picture made of animal bubbles: a cat
face, a fish, an owl, a cupcake, a rocket, a teddy bear and 136 more shapes. Every level has been checked by a
solver and can be won without boosters. After level 200, levels 101 to 200 come back with the animals mixed in a new way.**

[Download Pet Match on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6738354347)

## How hard is it?

The first levels are calm and teach the rules. The difficulty steps up at level 20 and then grows slowly.
In the first 19 levels two animals of similar colour never share a level, so they are easy to tell apart.

## Animals you match

Chicks, bunnies, bears, foxes, penguins, pandas, owls, elephants, giraffes, puppies, piglets, unicorns
and sheep: 13 animals in all.
