# Pet Match Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-15

This policy explains how Pet Match: Zoo Puzzle (the "App"), made by Stanislau Malchanau ("we",
"us"), handles your information. Short version: **your game progress stays on your device and
in your own iCloud account. We do not sell your personal data. The App shows ads from Unity, and
your advertising identifier is used for them only if you allow tracking.**

## 1. Data the App stores

The App stores the following on your device: the level you have reached, your boosters, which
tutorials you have seen, your purchases (for example whether ads are removed), and your
settings for music, sounds and vibration.

We do not run servers that receive this data, and we have no access to it.

## 2. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the App saves your progress through Apple's CloudKit, in the
private database of your own iCloud account, so it comes back on a new iPhone or iPad. This data
is stored and protected by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
We cannot see, read or export it. You can turn sync off in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

## 3. Advertising

From level 20 the App shows ads: banners, full-screen ads between levels, and videos you can
choose to watch for extra boosters. The ads are delivered by Unity Technologies through its
LevelPlay platform and ad networks (formerly ironSource). No other ad network is used.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Unity receives data from your device:
the IP address, the device model, the iOS version, language and region, the App's version,
an identifier for this app on your device (IDFV), and which ads were shown, tapped or watched.
Unity processes it under its own policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

**Tracking.** The App asks once, with Apple's standard prompt, whether it may track you. Only if
you allow it, Unity also receives your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and may use
it to show ads based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. If you do not
allow it, the identifier is not shared and the ads are not personalised that way. You can change
your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Pet Match.

Buying "No Ads" removes banners and full-screen ads. We do not receive any of the data above
from Unity, only totals such as how many ads were shown.

## 4. Purchases

Booster packs, "No Ads" and subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store.
We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the App only which purchases you own and
whether a subscription is active.

## 5. Usage analytics and crash reports

To learn which levels are too hard or too easy and where players get stuck, the App sends
anonymous usage signals to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service of TelemetryDeck GmbH (Augsburg,
Germany) with servers in the EU. A signal names an action in the game: for example, that a level
was started, won or lost (with its number and how many moves it took), that a booster or a
tutorial step was used, that an ad was shown, or that the shop was opened or a purchase was made
(with the product and its price). It comes with a timestamp rounded to the hour and general
technical details: the App and iOS versions, the device model, the language, region and time
zone, and display settings such as dark mode.

A signal never contains your name, email address, Apple Account, advertising identifier or
location. Your installation is identified only by a random identifier that the App salts and
hashes before sending, so the signals cannot be traced back to you or followed across apps.
TelemetryDeck stores no IP addresses and uses no cookies. We look at these signals only as
statistics to improve the game; we do not sell them or share them with advertisers.

Separately, if you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings > Privacy &
Security > Analytics & Improvements, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous usage statistics
and crash reports. They do not identify you.

## 6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminders to play on your device. No data is sent
to us to deliver them. You can turn them off in iOS Settings > Notifications > Pet Match.

## 7. Children

The App is rated 4+ and is a game for a general audience. It is not directed at children under
13 (or the equivalent age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information
from them.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

Your progress stays as long as you keep it. You can delete it at any time by deleting the App
and, for synced data, in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Pet Match.
Because we hold no copy, there is nothing further for us to delete. Usage signals are anonymous
and cannot be matched to you. Data Unity receives for ads is kept under Unity's policy linked in
section 3, which also explains how to ask Unity to access or delete it.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA in
California, APPI in Japan, PIPA in Korea or LGPD in Brazil), you may have the right to access,
correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to
withdraw consent. You can stop personalised ads at any time by turning tracking off (section 3).
You can also contact us with any request or question, and you may complain to your local data
protection authority.

We do not sell personal information. Under the CCPA/CPRA, letting Unity use the
advertising identifier for personalised ads may count as "sharing"; it happens only if you allow
tracking, and turning tracking off opts you out.

## 10. International transfers

Unity may process ad data in the United States and other countries. TelemetryDeck
processes signals in the EU.

## 11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell
you in the App.
