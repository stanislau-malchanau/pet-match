# Pet Match: Zoo Puzzle

Questions, a problem or an idea? Write to **7tam.official@gmail.com**. We answer every
message, usually within two days.

- **Restore purchases** on a new phone or after reinstalling: tap the gear button at the top
  of the game screen, then Restore Purchases.
- **Subscriptions:** manage or cancel them in iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.
- **Your progress** is saved on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own
  iCloud account, so it comes back on a new iPhone or iPad.
- **Ads** start at level 20. No Ads removes banners and full-screen ads.
